NFL Standings/Scoring

Projected record and division odds come from 2,000 simulated finishes to the regular season, using each team's record, its remaining games and a small home-field edge. Projected record is the average simulated finish, rounded. Projected regular season points = expected total wins (1 point each) + chance of winning the division × 5 points. Playoff points are not projected.
