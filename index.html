Team points and scoring rules

Each team earns 1 point per regular-season win, 5 for winning its division, 3 per playoff win, 5 for a conference title and 5 for the Super Bowl. An entry's score is the sum of its teams. Projections come from 2,000 simulated finishes to the regular season. A team's projected points are its current points plus its expected remaining wins and its chance of winning the division (shown as Division odds); playoff points are not projected. Proj. record is the average simulated finish. Movement compares with the end of last week. Ties are broken by total Super Bowl points. Prizes: 50%, 25% and 15% to the top three overall, 10% to the regular-season leader.
