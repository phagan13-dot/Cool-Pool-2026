Team points and scoring rules

Each team earns 1 point per regular-season win, 5 for winning its division, 3 per playoff win, 5 for a conference title and 5 for the Super Bowl. An entry's score is the sum of its teams. Ties are broken by total Super Bowl points. Prizes: 50%, 25% and 15% to the top three overall, 10% to the regular-season leader.
